I Take the Initiative to Learn
💡
Think carefully: What would happen if you stopped a good habit midway?
  • You planted a tree 🌱, watered it every day, then stopped — what happens?
  • You stopped feeding your pet 🐱 — what happens?
  • You started memorizing Qur'an 📖, then stopped after 3 months — what happens?
  • You started learning computers 💻, then stopped after one week — what happens?
✍️ Conclusion: Stopping a useful activity leads to loss, regression, and missing out on rewards.
2
I Read and Memorize — The Honorable Hadith
"That which is done continuously, even if it is small."
📜 Narrated by Imam Muslim — from A'isha (May Allah be pleased with her)
When asked: "What is the most pleasing act to Allah?"
أَدُومَهُ
Done Continuously
The continued, uninterrupted performance of good deeds.
وَإِنْ قَلَّ
Even If It Is Small
Even a little deed — size does not matter, consistency does.
3
I Understand the Indication
🕌
Key Lesson: Consistent small good deeds are better than interrupted great ones.
  • The most beloved deed is one a person continues doing, even if small.
  • Choose deeds you can do — so you can continue them without burden.
  • By doing deeds continuously, you will win the love of Allah ﷻ.
"Do only those good deeds which you can do, for Allah does not get tired (of giving rewards) till you get tired."
📜 Narrated by Al-Bukhari
4
I Read and Compare
❌ Khalid
Honored his mother for years, then grew bored and spoke hurtful words. She died heartbroken days later.

Result: Stopped good deed → great harm & regret.
✅ Jassim
Cared for elderly neighbor daily. Even after moving far away, he continued visiting every day.

Result: Kept up good deed → earned Allah's love & reward.
5
Benefits & Helpful Means
  • 🌟 Guidance by Allah ﷻ — for those who strive continuously.
  • 🐋 Surviving Adversity — Prophet Yunus's constant praise saved him from the whale. (Surat As-Saffat 143–144)
  • 💧 Purification — Five daily prayers erase sins like bathing 5 times removes all dirt. (Al-Bukhari)
  • ✈️ Reward Even When Unable — Illness or travel: you still receive reward for regular deeds. (Al-Bukhari)
  • 🌙 Making Up Missed Acts — Missing nightly recitation? Reading between Fajr–Zuhr counts. (Muslim)
